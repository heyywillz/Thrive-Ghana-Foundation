Welcome to Thrive Ghana Foundation

Empowering Communities. Building Leaders. Creating Lasting Change.

Thrive Ghana Foundation is a not-for-profit Ghanaian NGO dedicated to empowering women, girls, and marginalized communities, particularly in the five regions in the North, Zongo neighborhoods, and settler enclaves.

EST. 2023
What We Do

Transforming Potential Into Leadership Through Empowerment And Training.

Through our programmes, we nurture leadership, amplify voices, and create opportunities for women and girls to participate meaningfully in civic life and decision-making.

We believe strong communities are built when women and girls are equipped with the skills, confidence, and networks they need to drive lasting change.

Learn More

Vision & Mission

Vision

To create a future where women, girls, and marginalized groups are fully aware of their social and political rights and are empowered to actively participate in civic and community life.

Mission

To provide opportunities and platforms for women and girls to develop their capacities, strengthen leadership skills, and actively engage in decisions that impact their lives and communities.

Welcome Message from the Executive Director

“Leadership is not defined by position alone, it is about courage, voice, and impact.”

It is with great pride and deep conviction that I welcome you to this bold and visionary initiative to nurture champions from our communities for generations to come. Our mission to transform the narrative of our Northern, Zongo and settler communities in Ghana could not be more timely or urgent.

At Thrive Ghana Foundation, we believe in building a future where every woman and girl has the confidence, skills, and opportunities to rise, lead, and inspire. As Executive Director, I draw inspiration from the courage, resilience, and brilliance of women and girls from the five regions in the North, the Zongo communities and marginalised communities who continue to defy limitations and strive for excellence.

Our focus is to help ignite their confidence, break barriers, and amplify their voices, ensuring that they not only take their rightful places at decision-making tables but also lead with influence and purpose.

With great pleasure, I invite you to join us in this noble cause. Together, we can nurture voices, cultivate leadership, and spark transformative change that will echo across generations.

Warmly,

Hajia Amina Sulemana

Executive Director

Organisational Values

Our values are the guiding principles that shape our mission to empower women and girls across Ghana’s five northern regions and Zongo and settler communities. They define who we are, how we work, and the impact we seek to make.

Leadership

Through mentorship, training, and exposure to opportunities, we inspire them to lead with confidence, purpose, and integrity in their communities and beyond.

Empowerment

Our programmes build their skills, self-belief, and networks, enabling them to take control of their futures and become catalysts for progress in their societies.

Integrity

Every decision, partnership, and activity is anchored in accountability, respect, and service to the communities we represent.

Inclusivity

Our programmes are designed to be accessible, participatory, and reflective of the diverse realities of our target communities, regardless of social, cultural, or economic background.

Collaboration

We engage community leaders, development actors, and civil society organisations to co-create solutions that amplify impact and ensure long-term relevance.

Partnerships and Networks

We cultivate and sustain relationships with institutions and individuals who share our vision, leveraging shared values and expertise to expand opportunities and drive transformative outcomes.

Get In Touch
What is Thrive Ghana Foundation all about?

Thrive Ghana Foundation is a registered not-for-profit organization dedicated to leadership development and empowerment, with a focus on women, youth, and grassroots communities, especially in the Zongo and settler communities of Ghana.

How can I donate to support your work?

You can donate directly through our website’s Donate page using secure online payment options. Every contribution goes directly into supporting our projects, training programs, and community initiatives.

Do you accept volunteers?

Yes! We welcome volunteers who are passionate about leadership, community development, and empowerment. Simply visit our Contact page, complete the application form, and our team will connect with you.

Where does my donation go?

Your donations support leadership training, youth development, women empowerment programs, mentorship initiatives, and community development projects. We ensure transparency and accountability in how funds are used.

How do I become a partner with Thrive Ghana Foundation?

If your organization or business is interested in collaborating, please contact us via the Contact page. We believe in building strong partnerships for lasting community impact.

How can I benefit from your programs?

Our programs are open to women, youth, and community members in our operational target areas. Visit our Contact or Events page to find out how to get involved.
